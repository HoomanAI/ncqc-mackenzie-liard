## Third-party data

The raw workbooks include values derived from third-party products. Those
values stay under their providers' terms, which are compatible with
redistribution with attribution:

- Water Survey of Canada discharge (HYDAT and Wateroffice), Environment and
  Climate Change Canada, Open Government Licence – Canada,
  https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada
- ERA5-Land, Copernicus Climate Change Service, Licence to use Copernicus
  Products, https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/
- MODIS MOD13Q1 v006, NASA LP DAAC; NASA data carry no restrictions on reuse,
  https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/data/data-citation-and-policies/
